THE TOWN WHERE EVERY SHADOW HAS A COLOR - voice lines (ElevenLabs Adam, change audio pitch ON)

========== NARRATOR ONLY (speed 1.15x) ==========

In my town, everyone's shadow is a different color.

My mom's shadow is rose pink. The baker's is cinnamon brown. The mailman's is sky blue, and it waves before he does.

Every evening, when the sun goes down, the whole town walks through the square, and the ground turns into a rainbow.

Everyone's shadow, except mine.

I don't have one. Not even a gray one.

Doctors looked under the lamp. They looked behind me. They looked under my bed.

Nothing.

The kids at school call me see-through. When they play shadow tag, I'm never it. I'm never anything.

So every sunset, I watch the rainbow from my bedroom window.

Mom always says, "Sweetie, you're not missing anything."

But I knew I was missing something.

Then one day, I found a painting in the back of the library. The day our town was founded, two hundred years ago.

Everyone in the painting had a colored shadow.

Everyone except one kid, standing right in the middle, holding a lantern.

Under the kid, someone had written two words. The Lantern.

I didn't know what it meant. Until the night of the Sunset Festival.

The whole town was in the square. Every shadow dancing on the stones.

Then the big lamp in the clock tower went out.

And the whole town went dark.

Every colored shadow disappeared at once. People couldn't see their own feet. Someone started crying. Then everyone was scared.

But I could see.

Because light had never stuck to me, the dark didn't either.

I could see every face. Every step. Every scared little kid.

So I took my mom's hand. Then the baker's. Then the mailman's.

And one by one, I walked the whole town out of the dark.

When the lamp came back on, everyone was looking at me.

Mom hugged me so tight, her pink shadow wrapped around both of us.

She whispered, "I told you. You weren't missing anything."

Now every year at the Sunset Festival, I'm the one who climbs the clock tower and lights the lamp.

I still don't have a shadow.

But now I know why.

Somebody has to be the light.
