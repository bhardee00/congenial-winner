========== FILE 1: NARRATOR (speed 1.30x) ==========

A Karen tried to kick my baby brother out of the library.

Leo turned the page. Then he said one word.

Then she grabbed his rattle and dropped it in the book return.

Leo just looked at her. Then he slowly pushed a giant stack of books off the table.

Every single one hit the floor.

So Karen marched to the front desk and slammed the bell. Over and over.

A chair behind the desk slowly turned around.

It was Grandma. Wearing a badge that said head librarian.

Leo climbed onto the big story time chair, opened a book, and twenty kids sat down to listen.

Karen tripped over the book stack on her way out, and landed right in the bean bag pit.

The next day, there was a new sign by the door.

Quiet please. Except babies. Stamped with one tiny handprint.
