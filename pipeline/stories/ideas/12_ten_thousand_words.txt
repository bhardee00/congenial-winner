TEN THOUSAND WORDS (idea A, reference #2 style) - full script
N = narrator, others named.

1. NARRATOR: In my town, everyone is born with ten thousand words. When your counter hits zero, your voice is gone. Forever.
2. NARRATOR: The number glows right on your chest, so everyone knows exactly how much you've got left.
3. NARRATOR: So nobody here talks much.
4. NARRATOR: Mr. Henderson orders at the diner by pointing. Pancakes. Syrup. The waitress. She's his wife.
5. NARRATOR: The mayor gives his speeches on cue cards. Forty cards. The last one just says "Thanks."
6. NARRATOR: Grandma Pearl has nine thousand words saved. She says she's saving them for something important.
7. GRANDMA: Not yet.
8. NARRATOR: She's been saying that for thirty years.
9. NARRATOR: Then there's Mr. Gibbs. His counter says zero. He used his last word at a football game. The word was "fumble." It wasn't even a fumble.
10. NARRATOR: And then there's me.
11. NARRATOR: I narrated every video game I played. Out loud.
12. KID: And he's going for it! Oh! He missed!
13. NARRATOR: I gave my dog Biscuit live commentary every time he chased a squirrel.
14. KID: Biscuit's closing in! Five feet! He's up the tree! Biscuit loses again!
15. NARRATOR: I sang in the shower. Every song. Both parts.
16. NARRATOR: I thought ten thousand was basically infinity.
17. NARRATOR: It wasn't.
18. NARRATOR: On my eleventh birthday, I blew out the candles and looked down at my chest.
19. NARRATOR: Fourteen.
20. MOM: Fourteen? Fourteen?! What did you do with them?!
21. NARRATOR: That cost her eight words. She didn't care.
22. NARRATOR: After that, I stopped talking. No "good morning." No "thanks." Not even "bless you."
23. NARRATOR: At dinner, my little brother Max asked me for the ketchup.
24. MAX: Can you pass the ketchup?
25. NARRATOR: I just stared at him. I wanted to answer so bad.
26. NARRATOR: So I went to the library and got a book on sign language. I practiced every night in the mirror.
27. NARRATOR: Hello. Thank you. Help. And Max's favorite sign: freeze.
28. NARRATOR: Because Max and I had a game. When I made the sign for freeze, he had to stop and not move a muscle. Max was undefeated.
29. NARRATOR: Then came the block party.
30. NARRATOR: The whole town was there. Nobody was talking, of course. Just chewing.
31. NARRATOR: Max chased a balloon right into the street.
32. NARRATOR: Behind him, the ice cream truck started backing up. Beep. Beep. Beep.
33. NARRATOR: The driver couldn't see him. Max couldn't hear the beeping over the music.
34. NARRATOR: Fifty people saw it. Fifty people had words to spare. Nobody said a thing.
35. NARRATOR: Mr. Henderson pointed. Grandma Pearl opened her mouth. Then she closed it.
36. NARRATOR: So I ran. And I yelled.
37. KID: Max! Stop! Truck! Behind you! Max! Move! Please! Max! Max!
38. NARRATOR: Ten words. He didn't even turn around. The music was too loud.
39. NARRATOR: I had four left.
40. KID: Max! Look at me!
41. NARRATOR: On the last word, my voice just stopped. Like someone unplugged it. Zero.
42. NARRATOR: But Max turned around. He saw me.
43. NARRATOR: So I did the only thing I had left. I made the sign for freeze.
44. NARRATOR: Max froze. Mid-step. Not one muscle.
45. NARRATOR: The truck rolled past him. Six inches away.
46. NARRATOR: The driver finally saw him and slammed the brakes. Ice cream went everywhere.
47. NARRATOR: Max ran over and hugged me. Then he signed something back. He'd been practicing too. Thank you.
48. NARRATOR: My mom squeezed us so hard I would've said "ow," if I could.
49. NARRATOR: Then Grandma Pearl walked into the middle of the street.
50. GRANDMA: That boy saved a life with fourteen words. What are the rest of you saving yours for?
51. NARRATOR: Turns out, that was the something important.
52. NARRATOR: After that, the town changed.
53. NARRATOR: Everyone learned sign language. The mayor signs his speeches now. They're way shorter.
54. NARRATOR: Mr. Gibbs signs "fumble" at every game. It's still never a fumble.
55. NARRATOR: And me? I've got zero words left.
56. NARRATOR: But I've never been heard more.
