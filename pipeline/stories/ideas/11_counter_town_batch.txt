Counter-town ideas (style: reference #2, the step-counter short). Every character has a glowing chest counter.
Shape: rule + number -> town coping montage -> how I wasted mine -> the reveal -> practising a workaround
-> the sibling crisis while everyone freezes -> big countdown to 0 -> the workaround saves her -> "after that, the town changed".

A. EVERYONE IN MY TOWN ONLY GETS 10,000 WORDS
Rule: at 0 your voice is gone for good. The town: the mayor gives speeches with cue cards, the waiter points at the menu,
grandma has saved 9,000 words for "something important". I wasted mine yelling at video games and doing sports
commentary for my dog. On my birthday I had 14 left, and mom fainted. I started learning sign language from library
videos. Crisis: my little brother walks toward the street while an ice-cream truck reverses; the crowd just stares
because nobody wants to waste words. I yell "STOP! TRUCK! BEHIND YOU!..." while the counter goes 14 -> 0. On the last
word my voice cuts out, he can't hear me, so I sign "FREEZE" (our game). He freezes; the truck misses him.
Ending: now the whole town signs, and the mayor's speeches are silent and much shorter.

B. EVERYONE IN MY TOWN ONLY GETS 5,000 HUGS
Rule: at 0 your arms turn to stone. The town: people greet with elbow bumps, the hospital has a "hug donation"
jar, the high-five guy at the gym has stone arms. I wasted mine hugging every dog in the park. At 20 left, mom made me
wear oven mitts as a warning. I practised catching things with my feet. Crisis: my baby sister's swing snaps on the
playground tower and she falls; nobody reaches out. I catch her (counter 20 -> 0 as I squeeze), my arms turn to stone
mid-fall, so I land on my back and hold her up with my feet. Ending: the town now "hugs" with their feet and stone-armed
grandpa finally hugs grandma again.

C. EVERYONE IN MY TOWN ONLY GETS 2,000 CLIMBS
Rule: every stair, ladder and tree counts; at 0 your feet stick to the ground. The town: ramps everywhere, the
firefighters use a catapult, the old man lives on the first floor of a skyscraper. I wasted mine climbing the
school rope every day. On my birthday: 9 left. I practised using a trampoline. Crisis: my sister's kite drags her
up the water tower ladder and the ladder breaks under her; everyone just looks up. I climb 9 -> 0, my feet lock on
the last rung, so I jump back down onto the trampoline and catch her on the bounce. Ending: the town is full of trampolines.

D. EVERYONE IN MY TOWN ONLY GETS 1,000 SWIM STROKES
Rule: at 0 you sink like a rock in water. The town: the lifeguard sits on a floatie, kids cross the lake on a zip line,
the swim team is just one floating grandpa. I wasted mine racing ducks. 30 left. I practised floating on my back
and kicking. Crisis: my brother's raft drifts toward the waterfall; nobody goes in. I swim 30 -> 0, my arms go heavy,
so I float on my back and kick, towing the raft by its rope with my teeth. Ending: the whole town backstrokes.

E. EVERYONE IN MY TOWN ONLY GETS 500 LAUGHS
Rule: at 0 your face is stuck serious forever. The town: comedians are illegal, the clown works at the bank, babies
are the only ones who laugh freely. I wasted mine on cat videos. 3 left. I practised making other people laugh
instead. Crisis: during a thunderstorm blackout my baby sister is terrified and won't stop crying; the grown-ups
just stare. I do my best faces and laugh along (3 -> 0); my face locks serious, but I keep going with silly
dances until she laughs. Ending: everyone in town now spends their time making each other laugh instead of laughing themselves.
