Me|At three a.m., an old phone under my bed buzzed with a text from myself. It said, "Don't eat the fish sticks."
Me|We didn't have fish sticks. Then my mom made fish sticks for lunch.
Me|You see, a week earlier, I found that phone in my school's lost and found. Every text in it was from me. Dated tomorrow.
Me|Monday: "Bring an umbrella." It rained. Only on me.
Me|Tuesday: "Say no to Derek." Derek asked to borrow ten dollars. I said no. He was wearing my shoes.
Me|So I started using it to win.
Me|I aced a pop quiz. I won a raffle. I knew exactly when my little sister would prank me, so I pranked her first.
Sister|How did you know?!
Me|Then one night, the texts changed.
Me|"Go to the recital."
Me|My sister had a dance recital on Friday. I already told her I couldn't go. I had a video game tournament.
Me|I ignored it.
Me|The next text came an hour later. "GO TO THE RECITAL."
Me|Then: "Please."
Me|That's when I got scared. Future me doesn't say please.
Me|So on Friday, I skipped my tournament. I sat in the very back row with a sign that said "Go Lily."
Me|She came on stage. She looked out at the crowd. Mom and Dad were stuck at work. The seats were empty.
Me|Then she saw my sign.
Me|She stopped dancing. She ran to the edge of the stage to wave at me.
Me|One second later, a giant stage light fell. Right where she had been standing.
Me|Everybody screamed. The light smashed into pieces.
Me|Lily didn't even notice. She was still waving at me.
Sister|You came! You actually came!
Me|My hands were shaking. If I had stayed home, she would have been standing right there.
Me|That night, the old phone buzzed one last time.
Me|"Thank you."
Me|It never buzzed again. I think I finally did the one thing future me needed.
Me|Now I go to every recital. Every game. Every school play. Even the bad ones.
Me|Last night at three a.m., my real phone buzzed. A text from Lily.
Sister|Don't eat the fish sticks.
Me|I asked how she knew. She said, "Because I made them."
