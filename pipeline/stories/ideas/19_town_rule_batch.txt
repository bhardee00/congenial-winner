Ideas in reference #5 style ("The Town Parade": every step plays a note, except mine).
Calm first-person kid narrator, a town with a gentle magical rule, I'm the one exception, a discovery, a crisis where my
difference saves everyone, a hug with a parent, the town changes. ~2:00, warm golden light, plaza + houses + night window.

A. THE TOWN WHERE EVERYONE'S SHADOW IS A DIFFERENT COLOUR (mine has none)
Mom's shadow is rose pink, the baker's is cinnamon, the mailman's is blue. At sunset the plaza looks like a rainbow. Mine is
nothing - not even grey. Kids say I'm "see-through". At night I watch the shadows dance on the walls from my window. In the
library I find the town's founding painting: one child in the middle with no shadow, labelled "the Lantern". The great
lamp in the clock tower goes out during the festival; in the dark every coloured shadow vanishes and people panic - but I'm the
only one who can see in the dark, because light was never stuck to me. I lead everyone out of the square. Mom hugs me.
Now at every festival, I'm the one who lights the lamp.

B. THE TOWN WHERE EVERYONE'S WORDS FLOAT UP AS BUBBLES (mine sink)
Everyone's words rise as soap bubbles: Grandpa's are slow and huge, the twins' are tiny and fast. Mine fall to the ground and
roll away. I stopped talking. In the attic I find my grandma's journal: her words sank too - "Sinking words land where they're
needed." A storm pops every bubble in the sky; no message can reach the firefighters across the river - except mine, which
roll along the ground, across the bridge, right to their feet. The town brings me to the clock tower to say thank you.
Now people bend down to listen.

C. THE TOWN WHERE EVERYONE GLOWS WHEN THEY'RE HAPPY (I never have)
Mom glows peach, Dad glows gold, the whole parade glows at night. I've never glowed once, so everyone thinks I'm sad. Truth is,
I'm just happy in a quiet way. Then I find the old town rule-book: "Some lights are on the inside." At the winter festival the
power goes out and the glowing crowd is blinding - nobody can see the stairs on the frozen bridge. I can, because I don't glow.
I guide them across one by one. My little sister says I'm glowing. I'm not. But for the first time, it feels like it.

D. THE TOWN WHERE EVERY DOOR SINGS WHEN YOU OPEN IT (doors stay silent for me)
Every door in town hums a little tune when someone walks through - the bakery door plays a waltz, school doors play a march.
For me, every door is silent. The kids call me "the ghost". One night I find the clock tower door - it's never sung for anyone.
It opens for me, silently, onto stairs nobody's climbed in a hundred years, and the town's songs are written on the walls:
the tower has been waiting for someone quiet enough to hear it. I wind the old bell, and every door in town sings at once.
Now when I walk through a door, everyone else goes quiet to listen.
