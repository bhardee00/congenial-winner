A KAREN DIDN'T WANT MY BABY BROTHER AT THE WATER PARK (idea E, reference #3 style, more dialogue) - full script

1. NARRATOR: A Karen tried to kick my baby brother out of the water park.
2. KAREN: Excuse me! Is that a baby? In my pool?
3. ME: He's just floating.
4. KAREN: Diapers in the pool? That's disgusting!
5. NARRATOR: Then she grabbed his floatie and popped it with her fingernail.
6. KAREN: Oops. Guess he can't swim now.
7. NARRATOR: Max just looked at her. Then he kicked his little legs and splashed her right in the face.
8. KAREN: My hair! I just got it done!
9. MOM: It's a water park, ma'am.
10. NARRATOR: So Karen stomped over to our chairs and threw our towels on the ground.
11. KAREN: These chairs are for adults only.
12. NARRATOR: Then she marched up to the lifeguard tower and blew the whistle herself.
13. KAREN: Whoever's in charge, get down here! That baby needs to leave!
14. NARRATOR: The lifeguard climbed down and slowly took off her sunglasses.
15. NARRATOR: It was Grandma.
16. GRANDMA: Ma'am, that baby isn't a guest.
17. KAREN: Then what is he?
18. GRANDMA: He's the swim teacher.
19. NARRATOR: Max climbed all the way up the high dive, and did a perfect backflip.
20. KAREN: That's impossible!
21. GRANDMA: You're banned. You can take the slide out.
22. NARRATOR: Karen went down the big slide, and landed right in the kiddie pool.
23. NARRATOR: The next day, there was a new sign by the pool.
24. NARRATOR: No Karens in the deep end. Signed with one tiny wet footprint.

- Ending: the new sign with the tiny wet footprint; the park carrying on behind it.
