Me|I was dressed as a ghost, hanging from a chandelier, while an eight-year-old girl tried to feed me soup.
Girl|You look hungry.
Me|Ghosts don't eat.
Girl|That's why you look hungry.
Me|You see, two days earlier, a billionaire offered me fifty thousand dollars to haunt his mansion.
Billionaire|My daughter isn't scared of anything. Spiders. Thunder. Taxes. Nothing.
Me|Isn't that good?
Billionaire|She keeps going into the woods at night. Last week she came back with a bear.
Billionaire|She named him Kevin. Kevin lives in the guest room.
Me|All I had to do was scare her once. I shook his hand immediately. How hard could scaring a kid be?
Me|Night one. I hid in her closet in a white sheet. When she opened the door, I screamed, "Boo!"
Me|She screamed too. Then she hugged me.
Girl|I always wanted a ghost!
Me|She named me Gerald. She put me in pajamas. She made me sleep in the dog bed. The dog was furious.
Me|The next morning, her dad pulled me aside.
Billionaire|You were supposed to scare her. Not move in.
Me|Bro, she gave me a bedtime.
Billionaire|Just be scarier.
Me|She braided my sheet!
Me|Night two. I went bigger. Chains. Flickering lights. Spooky organ music.
Me|I rattled the chains down the hallway, and the bear walked out of her room.
Me|I screamed. The bear screamed. The girl ran out and stood in front of me.
Girl|Nobody scares Gerald!
Me|The bear apologized. I think. He went back to bed.
Me|Then the girl tucked me in. Next to the bear. Kevin snores.
Me|The billionaire looked like he was about to explode.
Billionaire|One more night. If she's not scared, you get nothing.
Me|So for the final night, he decided to help. He rented a fog machine, fifty skeletons, and a guy who plays scary violin.
Me|He even dressed Kevin as a vampire. Kevin did not want to be a vampire.
Me|At midnight, the lights went out. Fog filled the room. The skeletons popped out of the walls.
Me|The violin guy started playing, and the billionaire jumped out of a coffin.
Billionaire|Booooo!
Me|Then he tripped on his cape, fell back into the coffin, and the lid slammed shut.
Billionaire|I'm okay!
Me|The girl looked at him. Then she looked at me.
Girl|Dad, are you trying to scare me?
Me|The billionaire went silent.
Girl|Why?
Billionaire|Because you're not scared of anything. And I'm scared of everything. Especially losing you in those woods.
Me|I froze. The bear froze. Even the violin guy stopped.
Me|Then the girl walked over and held his hand.
Girl|Okay. I'll stop going in the woods. If you stop hiding in coffins.
Me|They hugged. The violin guy played something beautiful. The skeletons clapped.
Me|Kevin cried. I didn't know bears could cry.
Me|The billionaire handed me the check.
Me|But I never scared her.
Billionaire|No. But you scared me into talking to her.
Me|I smiled, took off my sheet, and headed for the door. "Thanks for the easiest fifty grand of my life."
Me|Then the bear walked out holding my pajamas.
Girl|Gerald, you forgot your blanket!
Me|I live here now. The bear and I share the dog bed.
