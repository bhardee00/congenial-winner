Me|I found a genie lamp at a yard sale for one dollar, and my grandma rubbed it before I could stop her.
Genie|You have three wishes!
Grandma|I wish you'd sit down. You're making me nervous.
Me|That was wish number one.
Me|You see, it was Grandma's yard sale. Everything was a dollar. Even Grandpa's recliner. Grandpa was still in it.
Genie|Technically, the lamp is hers. So the wishes are hers.
Me|Two wishes left. I had to make her use them right.
Me|I said, "Grandma, wish for a million dollars."
Grandma|I wish for a million coupons.
Me|The house filled with coupons. They were all for soup. The same soup.
Genie|One wish left. Choose wisely.
Me|I begged her. A mansion. A yacht. A lifetime of video games for her favorite grandson.
Grandma|I'll think about it.
Me|She thought about it for three weeks.
Me|The genie moved in. He slept on the couch. He ate all the soup.
Genie|Can she hurry up? I have other lamps.
Me|So I tried to help. I made her a list of good wishes. She used it as a coaster.
Me|I made a slideshow called "Wishes That Aren't Soup." She fell asleep on slide two.
Me|I even hired a guy to dress up as a yacht. She gave him soup.
Me|Then one night, I saw her standing next to Grandpa's recliner.
Me|Grandpa has bad knees. He hasn't gotten out of that chair in five years.
Me|She was holding the lamp. I thought, here it comes. The big wish.
Grandma|I don't need anything. I've had everything I wanted for sixty years.
Me|Then she handed me the lamp.
Grandma|You use it.
Me|I could have anything. A mansion. A yacht. A lifetime of video games.
Me|I looked at Grandma. I looked at Grandpa. I looked at the genie, who was eating soup.
Me|I wish Grandpa could dance with Grandma one more time.
Me|The genie dropped his spoon.
Genie|Now that's a wish.
Me|Grandpa stood up. Out of the recliner. On his own.
Me|He held out his hand. Grandma took it.
Me|They danced in the living room for an hour. On a floor covered in soup coupons.
Me|I didn't get a yacht. Best wish I ever made.
Me|The genie was supposed to leave after that. He didn't.
Genie|Is there more soup?
Me|He lives here now. Grandma charges him a dollar a night.
