Ideas in reference #4 style (power rule list, ~37 s, stat meter over the head, twist + reversal + "Comment ___" CTA)

A. EVERY TIME SOMEONE TELLS ME TO HURRY UP, I GET FASTER  (meter: SPEED)
Mom: "Hurry up, we're late!" - I'm buckled in the car before she finds her keys. Teacher: "Hurry up and finish your test." -
I finish it before she hands it out. Coach: "Hurry up!" - I lap the whole planet. Dud: Grandpa just taps his watch - "Tapping
your watch doesn't count." Bus driver: "Hurry up, kid." - I get to school before the bus. Twist: Grandma moves in. She says
"Take your time, sweetie" to everything. Reversal: now I'm slower than a snail; a snail passes me. CTA: "Comment HURRY UP if you
want me fast again."

B. EVERY TIME SOMEONE LAUGHS AT ME, I GROW A FOOT TALLER  (meter: HEIGHT)
The class laughs at my haircut - head through the ceiling tiles. The bully laughs - I step over his house. Dud: my sister just
smirks - "Smirking doesn't count." The whole school laughs at my talent show - I can see my house from the gym. Twist: Mom tells
the whole family to stop laughing; Dad tells his dad jokes and nobody laughs. Reversal: I'm the size of a hamster. CTA: "Comment
HAHA if you want me tall again."

C. EVERY TIME SOMEONE YELLS MY NAME, I TELEPORT TO THEM  (meter: ZAPS)
Mom yells from the kitchen - I appear next to her, mid-shower, in a towel. The teacher yells my name - I land on her desk. Dud:
Dad whispers it - "Whispering doesn't count." The coach yells it - I appear on the field mid-play, holding the ball. Twist: the
whole stadium chants my name at once. Reversal: there are now fifty of me, and Mom has to feed all of us. CTA: "Comment my name
and see what happens."

D. EVERY TIME SOMEONE SAYS "YOU CAN'T", I INSTANTLY CAN  (meter: POWER)
"You can't reach the cookie jar" - my arm stretches across the kitchen. "You can't fly" - I'm on the roof waving. "You can't beat
me at basketball" - the bully gets dunked on, literally. Dud: Grandpa just raises an eyebrow - "Eyebrows don't count." Twist: Mom
says "You can't clean your room in five minutes." Reversal: I did. Now she says it every day. CTA: "Comment YOU CAN'T."
