Me|I was upside down in a stranger's living room while a ninety-year-old grandma held me in a headlock.
Grandma|Who sent you?
Me|I couldn't tell her. Her son was hiding behind the couch, shaking his head at me.
Me|You see, the day before, a dad offered me ten thousand dollars to fake-rob his house.
Dad|My kids think I'm a coward. I just need to save them one time.
Me|So I break in, you punch me, I run?
Dad|Fake punch. I bruise easy.
Me|Apparently, his kids once watched him run away from a goose. The goose chased him into a lake.
Me|I shook his hand immediately. How hard could losing a fake fight be?
Me|Well, at eight p.m. I climbed through the window and yelled, "Nobody move!"
Me|The dad stood up, took a deep breath, and fainted.
Me|His kids stared at him. Then they stared at me.
Kid|Are you a robber?
Me|Uh, yes.
Kid|Cool. Do you want a juice box?
Kid|Can you rob us every night?
Me|That's not how robbing works.
Me|So I sat on the couch and waited for him to wake up. Forty minutes. We watched two cartoons.
Me|When he finally woke up, he dragged me into the hallway.
Dad|Be scarier this time.
Me|Bro, you fainted.
Dad|I was resting my eyes.
Me|Round two. I kicked open the front door with a flashlight under my face. "Give me all your snacks!"
Me|The dad grabbed a frying pan, swung it as hard as he could, and hit himself in the head.
Me|The kids gasped. I gasped. The frying pan was fine.
Grandma|Keep it down! My show is on!
Me|That was Grandma. Somewhere upstairs. Remember her.
Me|I caught him before he hit the floor, and his daughter screamed.
Kid|Mom! The robber is saving Dad!
Me|Now the whole family was clapping for me. The dad looked like he was about to explode.
Dad|Last chance. If I don't look like a hero, you get nothing.
Me|So for the final round, he made a plan. I'd grab the TV, and he'd tackle me on the way out.
Me|He practiced his hero pose in the mirror twelve times.
Me|I grabbed the TV. It weighed more than me. I took one step, the dad jumped across the room, and Grandma came out of the bathroom.
Me|She caught him in midair, threw him onto the couch, and grabbed me by the neck.
Grandma|Who sent you?
Me|Your son did!
Me|Everybody froze.
Kid|Dad, did you pay the robber?
Me|The dad went silent. His face turned bright red.
Dad|I just wanted you guys to think I was brave.
Me|His daughter walked over and hugged him.
Kid|You don't have to be brave, Dad. You just have to be here.
Me|Grandma let go of my neck. I almost cried. Partly because of the hug. Mostly because of my neck.
Me|Then the dad handed me the ten thousand dollars anyway.
Me|What's this for?
Dad|For being the worst robber in history.
Kid|Can you come back next week?
Me|As a robber?
Kid|As a babysitter.
Me|I smiled and walked to the door. "Thanks for the easiest ten grand of my life."
Me|Then Grandma tackled me through the window.
Me|I wasn't even robbing!
Grandma|Nobody robs this house twice.
