A KAREN TRIED TO KICK MY BABY BROTHER OUT OF THE LIBRARY (idea A, reference #3 style) - full script

1. NARRATOR: A Karen tried to kick my baby brother out of the library.
2. KAREN: Shh! Is that a baby? In my library?
3. ME: He's just reading.
4. KAREN: Babies can't read. That's ridiculous.
5. NARRATOR: Leo turned the page. Then he said one word.
6. LEO: Goo.
7. KAREN: Shh!
8. NARRATOR: Then she grabbed his rattle and dropped it in the book return.
9. KAREN: Oops. Quiet time.
10. NARRATOR: Leo just looked at her. Then he slowly pushed a giant stack of books off the table.
11. NARRATOR: Every single one hit the floor.
12. KAREN: I'm calling the librarian!
13. MOM: Ma'am, you're the loudest one here.
14. NARRATOR: So Karen marched to the front desk and slammed the bell. Over and over.
15. KAREN: Hello! I want the head librarian! Now!
16. NARRATOR: A chair behind the desk slowly turned around.
17. NARRATOR: It was Grandma. Wearing a badge that said head librarian.
18. GRANDMA: Ma'am, that baby isn't a visitor.
19. KAREN: Then what is he?
20. GRANDMA: He runs story time.
21. NARRATOR: Leo climbed onto the big story time chair, opened a book, and twenty kids sat down to listen.
22. KAREN: That's impossible!
23. GRANDMA: Shh. You're banned. The exit is that way.
24. NARRATOR: Karen tripped over the book stack on her way out, and landed right in the bean bag pit.
25. NARRATOR: The next day, there was a new sign by the door.
26. NARRATOR: Quiet please. Except babies. Stamped with one tiny handprint.
