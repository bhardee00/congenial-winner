Ideas in the style of reference #3 (supermarket Karen). Same formula: one place, a rude stranger picks on the baby,
three escalations, calm clever comebacks, a twist that the family secretly runs the place, Karen banned, a callback sign.
~45-60 s each (about 150-200 words).

A. A KAREN SAID MY BABY BROTHER WAS TOO LOUD FOR THE LIBRARY
Karen shushes baby Leo for saying "goo." She unplugs his rattle, then takes his stroller spot and puts her own
purse in it. Leo replies "goo goo gaga" and Mom side-eyes. Karen screams "I want the HEAD librarian!" Grandma walks out
from behind the desk with a "HEAD LIBRARIAN" badge: "Ma'am, the loudest thing in here is you." Karen is banned from
every library in town. The callback: a sign reading QUIET PLEASE (except babies), stamped with a tiny handprint.

B. A KAREN TRIED TO TAKE MY SISTER'S SEAT ON THE PLANE
Karen says babies shouldn't get window seats. She pushes the seat back onto Rosie's bottle, then hits the call button
40 times. Rosie throws her a pacifier like a tip. Karen demands to speak to the captain. The captain comes out: it's
Grandpa. "This is my granddaughter's plane. I named it after her." Karen rides the rest of the flight in the
luggage area with the suitcases. The callback: a "ROSIE AIRLINES - NO KARENS" sticker on the tail.

C. A KAREN CUT US IN LINE AT THE ICE CREAM SHOP
Karen cuts in front of the stroller: "Babies don't even eat ice cream." She orders 12 samples, then knocks over the baby's
cone. The baby blows a raspberry, sprinkles fly. Karen goes LIVE: "This shop serves CHILDREN." The owner steps out
from the back. It's Mom, in a pink apron with OWNER on it. Karen's live chat says "lol". Banned. The callback: a new flavour
on the menu board called "KAREN TEARS (salty)".

D. A KAREN TRIED TO GET MY BABY SISTER KICKED OUT OF THE PET STORE
Karen says, "Pets only, no babies." She calls the baby "a hamster," then tries to buy the puppy the baby is petting.
The baby barks back at her and the puppy growls at Karen. Karen yells for the manager. The manager is the store's
parrot, and it says "BANNED." Then the real manager (Dad) agrees. Karen is chased out by every animal at once.
The callback: a fish tank with a "NO KARENS" sign floating in it.

E. A KAREN DIDN'T WANT MY BABY BROTHER AT THE WATER PARK
Karen says, "Diapers in the pool? Disgusting." She steals his floatie, then takes the last lounge chair. Baby Max
splashes her with one tiny kick. Karen marches to the lifeguard stand and whistles for "whoever's in charge." The
lifeguard pulls off her sunglasses: it's Grandma. "Ma'am, he's the swim instructor." Max does a perfect backflip
off the diving board. Karen goes down the slide into the kiddie pool. The callback: a "NO KARENS IN THE DEEP END" sign
signed with a wet footprint.
